About Satkar Pure Point

Satkar Pure Point is an authorized Amul enterprise based in Indore, Madhya Pradesh. We specialize in delivering the full range of Amul dairy products — from fresh milk and butter to ghee, cheese, paneer, sweets, and ice cream — directly to your doorstep.


Our commitment is simple: pure products, fresh delivery, and honest service. Every product we deliver is handled with care through our cold-chain network to ensure it reaches you in perfect condition.
